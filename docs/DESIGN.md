# Design

CareCompanion is calm and plain. Seniors get large controls. Families get a short view of the day, with the rest one tap away. The same sage, coral, gold, and blue colors are used on the iPhone and the watch, in light and in dark.

Light is the watch default. Dark is used on the watch only when Dark is chosen in Settings. System follows the iPhone.

## Family iPhone

Home is a greeting, a one-line status for the selected senior, a tile for each senior, and a care-insight shortcut.

A senior's care page fits on one screen: name, status, check-in, mood, watch, and today's numbers. Medicines and the care insight stay closed until they are opened.

Profile shows the signed-in family member. Each senior is a closed row with age and city. Opening it edits that senior's details, mood times, contacts, medicines, and health baseline.

Messages sit apart from each other. Each person's name keeps one theme color.

A chosen mood is a pale green card on the iPhone and on the watch.

## Senior iPhone

One task fills the screen: check in, choose a mood, mark a medicine, read a message, or send an SOS. The mood check has no close button. The senior chooses Good, Okay, or Not great.

## Apple Watch

The home screen is four round tiles: Mood, New, Talk, and SOS.

New shows a medicine on a gold card and a visit on a blue card. The medicine card has an empty circle to mark the dose taken. Talk keeps a message button beside the microphone. A swipe from the left edge returns to the previous screen.
